Privacy Policy — ChessUnlock

Developer: Aimlessoul

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Summary: ChessUnlock helps you reduce distractions by restricting selected apps until you solve chess puzzles. Information needed to enforce app locks is primarily stored on your device. We use third-party services for chess puzzles, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, and Premium subscription management. We do not sell personal data ourselves. This policy explains what information these features may process and how you can manage it.

1) What ChessUnlock does

ChessUnlock lets you select apps to restrict, solve chess puzzles to unlock them, choose a temporary break duration, and track your puzzle progress. On Android, app locking uses device permissions and a blocking overlay. On iOS, it uses Apple Screen Time-related features, including FamilyControls and system app shields.

ChessUnlock also offers an optional auto-renewable Premium subscription with features such as longer unlock durations, additional emergency unlocks, and an ad-free experience while Premium is active. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, with RevenueCat used to manage subscription access.

2) Information processed by ChessUnlock

We do not ask for access to your personal messages, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location to provide the app-locking feature. Some third-party services may nonetheless process device identifiers, approximate location derived from an IP address, and other data described below.

2.1 Information stored on your device

To operate ChessUnlock, the app may store:

  • Selected apps: Android package names, app labels or icons, or iOS app/category/web-domain selection tokens used for blocking.
  • Locking information: Lock status, break-time selection, scheduled unlock deadlines, shielding state, and related settings.
  • Chess progress: Solved puzzle counts, best rating, accuracy, difficulty preferences, and other basic statistics.
  • Emergency unlocks: Usage counts and reset information needed to enforce Free and Premium limits.
  • App preferences and onboarding: Setup completion, selected onboarding answers (such as where you heard about ChessUnlock), themes, and other preferences.
  • Subscription state: Locally cached purchase/entitlement information so Premium features can be displayed and managed appropriately.

These records are generally held in on-device app storage, including Android preferences and iOS storage or App Group storage for Screen Time extensions. ChessUnlock does not intentionally send your exact selected-app list or Apple Screen Time selection tokens to our own servers.

2.2 iOS Screen Time and Android app-use permissions

On iOS, Apple may represent selected apps, categories, and websites as privacy-preserving tokens. ChessUnlock uses these selections to apply and remove restrictions and schedule relocking. It does not use Screen Time access to read messages or content inside other apps.

On Android, Usage Access can reveal information about which app is in the foreground. ChessUnlock uses that information to detect selected locked apps and enforce the blocking screen. We do not intentionally send a full history of your foreground app use to our own servers.

2.3 Premium subscriptions and purchases (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to manage Premium subscription access on Android and iOS. RevenueCat and the applicable app store may process information such as:

  • A RevenueCat App User ID, which may be automatically generated and anonymous or pseudonymous.
  • Subscription product identifiers, purchase/transaction identifiers, store or platform, and purchase or renewal dates.
  • Subscription status, expiration dates, cancellations, refunds, billing issues, and restored purchases.
  • Technical and diagnostic data needed to provide and troubleshoot the subscription service.

ChessUnlock uses this information to confirm whether Premium is active, unlock paid features, restore purchases, and remove Premium access when an entitlement expires. Purchases and recurring billing are processed by Apple or Google, not directly by ChessUnlock. We do not receive your full payment-card number or card security code.

Canceling a subscription or uninstalling ChessUnlock does not automatically erase transaction records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat. To manage or cancel a subscription, use the app's Manage Subscription option or the relevant app store.

2.4 Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how ChessUnlock is used and improve reliability and features. Events may include app launches, onboarding progress, number of apps selected, lock screens shown, puzzles started or solved, extra puzzles, Hint/Skip usage, difficulty changes, break-time selection, and subscription-related interactions.

We do not intentionally include your exact locked-app names, package names, iOS selection tokens, contacts, or personal messages in analytics events. Firebase may separately process app-instance identifiers, device information, session information, approximate location, and advertising identifiers where permitted and available.

2.5 Crash and diagnostic data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose errors. Crash and diagnostic reports may include stack traces, recent app events, app version, operating-system version, device model, installation identifiers, and technical details associated with a failure.

2.6 Advertising and consent choices

Free users may see Google AdMob banner ads and rewarded ads, including ads used with Hint and Skip features. AdMob and its partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostic information for purposes such as serving and measuring ads, fraud prevention, and security.

Premium subscribers do not receive in-app advertisements while Premium is active. Other services, including analytics, crash reporting, and subscription management, may still operate as described in this policy.

Where applicable, ChessUnlock uses Google's consent-management tools to request and respect advertising privacy choices. If a privacy-options control is shown in the app, you can use it to review or change your choices. You may also manage device-level advertising and tracking preferences through Android or iOS settings. The availability of particular choices depends on your region, platform, and applicable law.

2.7 Feedback and support

If you contact us for support or feedback, we may receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and relevant device information. Providing feedback is optional.

3) Permissions and why we request them

3.1 Android

  • Usage Access: To identify when a selected app is in the foreground.
  • Display over other apps: To show the blocking overlay over a selected app.
  • Foreground Service and notifications: To support ongoing app monitoring and display required service notifications.
  • Internet: To retrieve puzzle content when needed and communicate with subscriptions, ads, analytics, diagnostics, and support services.
  • Advertising ID, where available: To support advertising and measurement by third-party SDKs, subject to applicable controls.
  • Boot completed, if enabled: To resume app monitoring after a device restart, subject to device restrictions.

3.2 iOS

  • Screen Time / FamilyControls: To let you choose apps, categories, or websites for restrictions.
  • ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity: To apply shields and support timed relocking.
  • Internet: For puzzle content when needed, app-store subscription services, ads, analytics, diagnostics, and support.
  • Tracking or advertising permissions, where applicable: To support advertising features permitted by iOS and your settings.

4) Third-party services

These services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • RevenueCat: Subscription identification, purchase records, entitlement checks, and subscription status. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: Payments, purchase history, subscriptions, cancellation, and refunds. Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: Ads, advertising consent, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google Advertising Privacy.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: Usage analytics and crash diagnostics. Firebase Privacy Information.
  • Lichess, if puzzle requests are made to its API: Chess puzzle delivery and ordinary network request processing. Lichess Privacy Policy.
  • Email and support providers: Messages or attachments you voluntarily submit.

5) How information is used

  • To save your app selections, enforce locks, and provide timed unlocks and emergency access.
  • To deliver chess puzzles and display local progress.
  • To verify, activate, restore, and manage Premium subscriptions.
  • To display ads to eligible Free users and respect applicable ad privacy choices.
  • To measure usage, identify technical problems, improve performance, and prevent abuse.
  • To respond when you contact us for help.

6) Data sharing and international processing

We do not sell personal information ourselves. We share or make information available to service providers only as needed for the functions described above. Third-party advertising providers may process data for advertising purposes under their own policies and your applicable privacy choices.

Selected app lists and iOS Screen Time tokens are intended to remain on your device. Subscription records, analytics, advertising data, and crash reports may be processed by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, and their service providers in countries other than the one where you live, subject to applicable protections and their policies.

7) Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can usually remove locally stored ChessUnlock data by clearing app storage on Android or uninstalling ChessUnlock. This may reset settings and app selections. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an active subscription; manage subscriptions through Apple or Google.

Analytics, advertising, crash, and transaction records stored by third parties may be retained according to their own retention schedules and legal obligations. Store transaction records may need to be retained for billing, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restrictions on the processing of personal data. Contact us at the address below to submit a privacy request. We may need limited information to verify a request and may direct you to Apple, Google, or another provider for records that they control.

You can also manage advertising and tracking choices through device settings and, where provided, the app's privacy-options control.

8) Security

We aim to minimize data collection and store app-locking information on the device where practical. Third-party services use their own technical and organizational security safeguards. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9) Children's privacy

ChessUnlock is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us through support or another channel, please contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

10) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our app, subscriptions, advertising, data practices, or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently updated.
